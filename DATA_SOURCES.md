# Data Quality Agent: data sources

Where every value the app shows or checks comes from. GlobalCorp SE and everything in its data are fictional: no client data, no live data and no outside service at run time. What is real is the method around the data: the VAT formats, the check-digit rules, the data-quality dimensions, the exchange rate and the SAP tables the data products stand in for. Each is listed here with its source and the date it was read. Version 2.0.0, 20 November 2026: the sources are the ones read for version 0.1.0 on 19 November 2026, each with the date in its row; later versions added no outside source.

## 3. VAT ID formats (rule DQ-10)

DQ-10 checks that a supplier's VAT ID exists, starts with its country's prefix and follows the country's format after spaces, dots, hyphens and the Swiss suffix are removed. It never checks a check digit. `js/vat.js` holds the formats; `tests/fixtures/vat.json` one passing and one failing example per country.

| Country | Prefix | Format | Source | Read | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Germany | DE | 9 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| France | FR | 2 letters or digits, then 9 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| United Kingdom | GB | 9 digits, 12 digits, or GD or HA + 3 digits | HMRC VAT manuals VATGPB9400 and VATREG12450 (b) for 9 digits and the GD and HA numbers; 12 digits (branch traders) only through a secondary page quoting HMRC's API, whose developer site refuses automated reading | 2026-11-19 | partly verified |
| Netherlands | NL | 9 digits, B, 2 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| Austria | AT | U + 8 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| Italy | IT | 11 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| Spain | ES | a letter or digit, 7 digits, a letter or digit; not digits at both ends | VIES structure table (a), remark 4: the first and last characters may be letters or digits, but not both digits (correction B1) | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| Switzerland | CHE | 9 digits, printed CHE-123.456.789 with MWST, TVA or IVA | Swiss UID ordinance SR 431.031, Art. 5, and `https://www.ch.ch/en/value-added-tax-number-und-business-identification-number/` | 2026-11-19 | verified |
| Greece (test case only) | EL | 9 digits | VIES structure table (a) | 2026-11-19 | verified |

(a) The European Commission's VIES FAQ, item 11 (`https://ec.europa.eu/taxation_customs/vies/faq.html`), renders empty without a browser; its structure table was read as the French customs reproduce it: "VIES — Structure des identifiants TVA", `https://www.douane.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/2021-02/02/VIES_Structure_des_identifiants_TVA_DEB_2021.pdf`, February 2021.
(b) HMRC's internal manuals on gov.uk, sections VATGPB9400 (VAT Government and Public Bodies) and VATREG12450 (VAT Registration).

**Stricter than a format** (correction B7). The published rules of section 4 add conditions a format check does not make: a German number never starts with 0, GD numbers run from 000 to 499 and HA numbers from 500 to 999. DQ-10 keeps to the format, as the method says; the world's builder and checker apply the full rules.

## 4. Check digits (the world's builder and checker only)

Every fictional VAT ID in the world must fail its country's check digit. The rule DQ-10 never uses these (section 3). `js/vat_check.js` implements them; `tests/world.mjs` checks every VAT ID in the world against them and proves on a planted number that a passing ID is caught.

| Country | Rule | Official? | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany | ISO 7064 MOD 11,10 over the nine digits; the first digit is not 0 | published | (c) |
| Austria | The eight digits after U: Luhn sum of the first seven, check digit = (6 − sum) mod 10 | published | (c) |
| Italy | Luhn over the eleven digits; office code (digits 8 to 10) 001 to 100, 120, 121, 888 or 999 | published | (c) |
| Netherlands | Modulo 11 as for the citizen number, or since 2020 ISO 7064 MOD 97-10 over "NL" and the number | published | (c) |
| United Kingdom | Weights 8 7 6 5 4 3 2 10 1, sum modulo 97 equal to 0, or 42 or 55 for numbers from 100 0000 00 (the 9755 rule); GD 000 to 499, HA 500 to 999 | published | (c) |
| Switzerland | Weights 5 4 3 2 7 6 5 4 over the first eight digits, check digit = 11 − sum mod 11; a result of 10 is never issued | published in a withdrawn standard | (d) |
| France | The key from the SIREN (numeric keys), or the letter-key procedure | not published (e); the rule in wide use (f) | (f) |
| Spain | The control character of the NIF: for entities the Luhn-derived digit or letter, for persons the DNI or NIE letter | not published (e); the rule in wide use (f) | (f) |
| Greece | Weights by powers of two, modulo 11 | not published (e); the rule in wide use (f) | (f) |

(c) Austrian Federal Ministry of Finance, "UID-Konstruktionsregeln", Stand November 2020: the construction rules each member state released for publication, `https://bmf.gv.at/dam/jcr:d6794f8f-d321-43df-9840-1a841f9bf5dc/BMF_UID_Konstruktionsregeln_Stand_November%202020.pdf`, read 2026-11-19.
(d) eCH-0097, version 5.2.0, `https://www.ech.ch`, read 2026-11-19; eCH withdrew the standard on 2 July 2021.
(e) The same document (c): France, Spain and Greece did not agree to publish their rules.
(f) python-stdnum 2.2, `https://pypi.org/project/python-stdnum/`, read 2026-11-19: its modules `fr.tva`, `es.nif` and `gr.vat` are the source of the three unpublished rules. `js/vat_check.js` agrees with python-stdnum 2.2 (also `de.vat`, `at.uid`, `it.iva`, `nl.btw`, `gb.vat` and `ch.uid`) on 37,795 generated numbers across all nine countries, without a disagreement (correction B2).

## 5. Data-quality dimensions

The rules use five of the six dimensions that the UK Government Data Quality Hub credits to DAMA UK: completeness, consistency, validity, uniqueness and timeliness. Accuracy is left out on purpose: whether a value matches reality needs an outside check that the prototype cannot make. Source: "Meet the data quality dimensions", `https://www.gov.uk/government/news/meet-the-data-quality-dimensions`, 24 June 2021, read 2026-11-19.

## 6. SAP tables behind the data products

The world ships as five data products and one log, standing in for standard views over SAP data. The tables are the usual sources; SAP's own documentation sits behind a sign-in and was not reached, so each field below is marked as the method requires.

| Data product | One row per | Typical SAP source |
|---|---|---|
| `po_line` | Purchase-order item | EKKO, EKPO |
| `contract` | Outline agreement (contract) | EKKO and EKPO with document category K |
| `supplier` | Supplier account | LFA1, LFB1, LFM1 (business partner in S/4HANA) |
| `material` | Material | MARA, MAKT |
| `user_org` | User | user master and organisation |
| `exception_log` | Exception ever detected | the agent's own store |

- The order item's contract reference: EKPO-KONNR, "Number of Principal Purchase Agreement" — typical source field, not verified.
- The requester: AFNAM, "Name of Requisitioner/Requester", a 12-character text on the requisition (EBAN) and the order item (EKPO), not a link to a user — typical source field, not verified. SAP note (KBA) 2955651, said to describe that a manually created requisition fills it with the user ID, was not reached. In the world a line's requester holds a user id, and a value that matches no user counts as missing (DQ-04).
- Third-party dictionary mirrors agree on both fields (`https://leanx.eu/sap/table/eban/`, `https://www.se80.co.uk/sap-data-element/?name=afnam`, `https://www.sapdatasheet.org/abap/dtel/konnr.html`, read 2026-11-19); they are not SAP's documentation.

## 7. The method's parameters

The fix paths and the financing cost use the parameters in `js/params.js`, the method's defaults (decision D4). They are assumptions, labelled as such wherever they appear, not measurements. Every run, live or simulated, uses the defaults; version 2.0.0 removed the Method dialog that could change them for Live runs (TECH_NOTES §6). The defaults: a day of work €1,200 at 8 hours; a mass change 0.5 days to set up, 2 minutes per record and 5 days' lead time; a follow-up 0.25 days to prepare, 10 minutes per owner and a 14-day window; a fix at the source 3 days with 20 days' lead time; a cost of capital of 6 % a year, invoices paid on the order's terms; a window of 365 days.

## 8. What the app does not use

No client data, no live company data, no web search and no outside service while it runs: the page loads its data from its own `data/` folder, and talks only to the Claude API, with your key, in Live mode.
