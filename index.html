    Show it in five minutes

    Four steps, five minutes. It plays by itself and stops where you act; Space pauses while you talk.

    1. The tip: point at the four figures. “€10,293 a year at stake, 23 records, a flag 62 days old.” 1 min
    2. Start the agent: Spot, Check, Calculate. “It suspects a supplier, then finds one buying profile behind all 23. Paying early cost €3,482 last year.” 2 min
    3. Propose: approve C. “You decide, not the AI: at stake drops to €0.” 1 min
    4. Prepare: approve both drafts. “Nothing leaves without a person. Live, this run costs about €0.10.” 1 min
