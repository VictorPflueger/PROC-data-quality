    How the prototype works

    Turns a vague tip into a costed fix you approve

    Bad purchasing data costs money, and nobody owns it. The agent finds the records behind a tip, why they are wrong, who can fix them and what each fix costs. A person decides.

    Five steps, and a person decides twice

    Fifteen rules check the data. The agent works out the cause; a rule engine, not the AI, computes every figure. Simulation plays a scripted run through the same tools; Live runs Claude on your key.

    1. Spot. The rules flag the records behind the tip.
    2. Check. The agent tests a cause, corrects itself when the data disagree and names who must act.
    3. Calculate. The engine works out what the case costs.
    4. Propose. Fix paths next to doing nothing. You decide.
    5. Prepare. Drafts wait for your approval. Nothing is ever sent.

    Four figures show what the agent brings

    They sit top right and change as you step through. The engine computes them, never the AI; only Session cost is an estimate in a simulation.
